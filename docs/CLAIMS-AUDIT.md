# Website Claims Audit

Last reviewed: 3 August 2026

The code-level source of truth is [`lib/claims.ts`](../lib/claims.ts). Approved wording is imported
from that registry into the English and Arabic content so the same claim cannot drift between pages.

## Publishing Rules

- Kemora is described as a manufacturing, product-development and sourcing partner. The website
  does not imply that Kemora owns every factory in its network.
- A partner factory's certificate is identified as belonging to that facility, not to Kemora.
- Capacity is described as high without publishing an unsupported daily figure.
- MOQ is a starting point, not a guarantee that every construction can run at that minimum.
- Customs, transit, testing and compliance remain conditional on the product and shipment.
- No client name, testimonial, review or campaign image is published without permission.

## Removed or Prohibited Claims

The following claims must not return without documented evidence and a fresh review:

- `5,000+ pieces per day` or any other exact network-wide capacity figure.
- `Our factory`, `our factories` or `our port` unless ownership is legally documented.
- `0% EU duty`, `save 12%`, `every shipment` or any guaranteed customs treatment.
- Fixed delivery promises such as `14-day delivery to Poland`.
- Blanket statements that every material is compliant or tested.
- `ISO 9001 certification in progress` or a partner certificate presented as Kemora's certificate.
- `20+ years of Kemora experience` until the registered business history is documented.
- Unnamed social proof such as `trusted by leading European brands`.
- Reviews, ratings, locations, certifications, client names or order volumes that cannot be verified.

## Portfolio and Image Provenance

Every gallery asset must be classified as one of:

- **Selected Project Work**: a completed Kemora client project with display permission.
- **Sample Development**: a physical development sample, not represented as bulk production.
- **Concept / Reference**: visual direction only, never described as Kemora production.

Two Meejhu campaign images remain withheld because visible third-party campaign branding requires
clear written permission even where Kemora produced the garments. Permission for the small visible
mark on the published grey sample was confirmed by the owner on 3 August 2026. The remaining gallery
records retain explicit labels. Keep written client approval for any identifiable brand campaign or logo.

## Privacy and Corporate Details Still Requiring Confirmation

The revised privacy notice accurately describes the current form, file uploads, Vercel hosting and
analytics, Resend email delivery and WhatsApp contact. It identifies Kemora only as the website brand
and intentionally omits an unconfirmed legal entity and registered address. The owner should confirm
with counsel:

1. Kemora's exact contracting/legal entity name.
2. The registered business address suitable for the privacy notice and commercial documents.
3. A written retention schedule for enquiries, attachments and project records.
4. Whether a formal EU or UK data-protection representative is legally required and appointed.
5. Whether an independently reviewed Arabic privacy notice is required.

## Operational Evidence Checklist

Maintain a private due-diligence folder containing:

- partner factory certificate copies, scopes and expiry dates;
- sample and bulk lead-time records by product category;
- MOQ feasibility notes by fabric and construction;
- inspection procedures and recent QC reports;
- supplier declarations and material test reports;
- recent export documents and eligible EUR.1 examples;
- image, case-study and client-name publication permissions;
- current shipping contacts and destination feasibility records.

This file is a content-control record, not legal or customs advice. Eligibility and compliance must
always be confirmed for the specific facility, product and shipment.
